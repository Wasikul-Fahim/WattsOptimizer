Keeping Shapla Shopping Complex alive through a day of load shedding The story It is 9:40 in the morning and Rokeya Apa, secretary of the Shapla Shopping Complex market committee in Mirpur, is standing outside the generator room holding the DESCO load-shedding schedule for today: 12 noon to 4 PM, and 5 PM to 8 PM. Seven hours without grid power on a Thursday. Shapla has one generator. Monir bhai, who runs it, has told her twice: "Apa, it's a 20-kilowatt machine. The shops in this building together want nearly forty. Put them all on and it trips. And we have seventeen litres of diesel. Seventeen. The tanker is not coming before Saturday." So for every outage hour, somebody has to decide who stays on and who sits in the dark. The pharmacy's insulin fridge and the bank's ATM booth are on every hour, no argument. The other ten shops all want power all day, and each has a case: Jamal the tailor has Eid orders; the mobile-phone shop is empty until 4 PM and packed from 5 to 8; the photocopy shop does its whole day before 3; the restaurant needs lunch and dinner and draws more than anyone; the electronics showroom sells after 5; the beauty salon has a wedding party booked 1 to 4; the grocery just wants its fridge; the jewellery, clothing and shoe shops want the evening crowd; Kashem's tea stall draws one kettle and one fan and wants to be left alone. Two things make it hard. The generator burns diesel per hour
, by how heavily it is loaded - not per shop. And no shop may be left dark three hours in a row
; two hours people grumble, three and the owner is in her office with his brother-in-law. Last year she did this with a pencil. The diesel ran out at 6:30 PM and the first floor went black in the evening rush. This year she has asked you. What you are building A web app that reads today's situation and produces the roster: for each outage hour, which shops are powered and which are dark, how much diesel is used, and why each dark shop is dark. You have 3.5 hours. At minute 150 everyone receives one updated input (DESCO extended the afternoon block). Re-run your app on it. What makes a strong entry? Powering the most valuable shops hour by hour is the obvious start, and it fails: it spends the diesel at noon and leaves the evening dark, and it starves the same low-value shop until the three-hours rule breaks. Strong tools treat the day as one plan, compute fuel from the combined load per hour, and can explain every dark hour with the real rule or trade-off. A correct simple method is enough; a clear screen is required, decoration is not. A worked example A small market - five shops, a 10 kW generator, 8 litres - so the formats are clear. How to read it: • grid_available - ten entries, one per hour from 10:00. Here the grid is off from 12:00 to 15:00 and from 17:00 to 20:00 (the false entries): six outage hours. Everything else is grid time, where nothing is decided. • generator_capacity_kw: 10 - in any outage hour, the kW of the powered shops added together must be ≤ 10. • fuel_table - litres per hour by combined load: up to 4 kW costs 0.80 L, up to 7 kW 1.20 L, up to 10 kW 1.60 L. Pharmacy + tea stall = 4 kW → 0.80 L. Pharmacy + tailor + photocopy + tea stall = 10 kW → one lookup, 1.60 L (not four separate ones). • fuel_budget_litres: 8.00 - the day's diesel. The six outage hours together must stay within it. • max_consecutive_dark: 2 - a shop may be dark two hours in a row, never three. Grid hours reset the count. • shops - the pharmacy (3 kW) is mandatory, so it is on in every outage hour and 7 kW remain for the other four. Each value_per_slot has ten numbers, one per hour: the mobile shop is worth 1 at 10:00 and 8 at 18:00; the photocopy shop is the reverse. Read it: the tea stall (1 kW) is never cut, it is nearly free. The mobile shop gets 18:00 and 19:00, its best hours. 14:00 runs almost empty to save diesel for the evening. No row has three ✗ in a row. The output JSON for this roster is example_output.json in your pack. The rules 1. Hours. Slots 0–9 are 10:00–20:00. A grid-on hour: everyone powered, no fuel, no roster entry, no value counted. A day with no outages is valid: empty roster, zero totals. 2. Shops. At most twelve. Unique non-blank id
, positive whole-number kw
, type mandatory or normal
, ten whole-number values ≥ 0. An empty shop list is valid. 3. Capacity. In every outage hour, the kW of all powered shops added together ≤ capacity. A shop bigger than the generator can never be on during an outage (it runs on the grid like everyone else). 4. Fuel. The table lists load levels (whole kW, increasing, last one = capacity) with litres per hour. Add up the powered kW in the hour, find the first level that is at least that much, charge its litres - once per hour on the combined load, never per shop
. Zero load = zero fuel. The day's total must be ≤ the budget. Work in whole hundredths of a litre; no rounding tolerance. 5. Mandatory. Every mandatory shop is on every outage hour. If no roster can satisfy all the rules together, answer status: infeasible and say which rule and which shops/hours are in conflict. A labelled best-effort roster may be attached. 6. Dark runs. For each normal shop, walk the ten hours in order: a dark outage hour adds one to its run; an on hour or a grid hour resets it to zero. No run may exceed max_consecutive_dark
. The day starts with a run of zero. dark_hours counts all dark outage hours, not the longest run. Mandatory shops have zero. 7. Objective. Value = sum, over outage hours, of value_per_slot[hour] for every powered shop (mandatory included). Maximize it under rules 3–6. Equal-value rosters are equally good. 8. Output. status is ok
, infeasible or invalid_input
. For ok
: every outage hour exactly once; powered and dark together are exactly the shop ids, no overlap, no repeats; every dark entry has a real reason (the rule or trade-off, with numbers); totals recomputed from the roster. invalid_input returns a message naming the field and exits non-zero (HTTP 400). 9. Invalid input. Anything that does not match the format in schema.md - a missing or mistyped field, wrong list lengths, duplicate ids, a bad fuel table, negative numbers, more than two decimals in litres - is invalid_input
. A valid file whose rules cannot all be met is infeasible
, not invalid. Who Stays On? · page 5
